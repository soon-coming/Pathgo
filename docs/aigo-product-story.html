06 · CULTURE, NOT JUST LITERALS

Aigo explains what the words may really mean.

Korean communication can be indirect. Aigo helps users read between the lines, flags uncertainty, and suggests a respectful next response—without pretending interpretation is absolute.

Literal translation and real-world interpretation are kept visibly separate.
Culture notes reduce friction while preserving the user’s agency.
